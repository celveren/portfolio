LIGHTSAGE to CÉLVEREN

New name.
New address.

LightSage is now Célveren. If you use a product at lightsage.dev, update its web address to use celveren.dev.

Change the domain. Keep the rest.

Replace lightsage.dev in the website’s domain with celveren.dev. Keep https://, any product name before the domain, and everything after the domain exactly as they are.

Old address New address
lightsage.dev celveren.dev
lightning.lightsage.dev lightningbot.app
udb-api.lightsage.dev udb-api.celveren.dev

Lightning is an exception: lightning.lightsage.dev now lives at lightningbot.app. Use the link above to visit its new home.

For example, https://product.lightsage.dev/settings becomes https://product.celveren.dev/settings. This example shows the address format; use your product’s actual name and page.

Your quick checklist

  1. Open the updated address. Change the domain in your browser’s address bar and visit the page.
  2. Update saved links. Replace old bookmarks, favorites, and shortcuts with the new address.
  3. Use the new address when sharing. Update links you’ve saved in messages, documents, or your own website.

Having trouble opening a page?

Check that the new domain is spelled celveren.dev (without an accent), and that you kept the product name and the rest of the address. If a saved page won’t load, try the product’s home page on the new domain and navigate from there.
